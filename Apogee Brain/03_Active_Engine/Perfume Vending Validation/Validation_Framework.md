## Evidence discipline
Use one label for each record. Do not upgrade a claim without supporting evidence.

- FACT: Directly verifiable information with a source.
- OBSERVATION: Something directly observed, without inferring demand.
- HYPOTHESIS: A proposition to test.
- ASSUMPTION: An unverified input used only to frame research.
- CUSTOMER EVIDENCE: Evidence from customer behavior or a customer conversation; record the source and context.
- MARKET EVIDENCE: Independent market evidence; record the source and scope.
- SUPPLIER CLAIM: A claim made by a manufacturer or supplier, pending independent verification.
- VALIDATED: Reserved for evidence that meets the agreed validation criteria. No item is VALIDATED at setup.

The existence of perfume vending machines elsewhere shows that the concept exists. It does not show that a Kenyan opportunity is validated.
