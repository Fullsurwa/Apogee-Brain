# Perfume Vending Customer Interview Questions

## Customer-problem questions

1. Have you ever wished you had perfume or fragrance with you when you didn't?
2. Tell me about the last time that happened.
3. What did you do instead?
4. How much of an inconvenience was that for you?
5. Have you ever bought perfume mainly because you wanted to smell good for a particular occasion?
6. Have you ever wanted to try a fragrance before buying the whole bottle?

## Proposed-solution questions

1. What do you think about that idea?
2. Where would you expect to see something like that?
3. What would make you actually use it?
4. What would make you not use it?
5. What would feel like a reasonable price for a few sprays?
6. What kind of fragrances would you expect it to have?
