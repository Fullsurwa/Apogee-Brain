# EvalLite Brain
### Prove your impact. The lite M&E + ROI engine for teams without a data department.

> **Measure outcomes. Link spend to impact. Forecast what's next.**
> Donor-grade evidence and ROI clarity — from a mobile-friendly dashboard, no data team required.

---

## The Problem
SMEs and NGOs are asked to *prove* results — to donors, boards, and investors — but they're stuck between spreadsheets that only look backward and enterprise BI tools (Power BI, Tableau) that are too heavy, costly, and technical. So spend gets justified on **anecdote, not evidence**, and the next budget is a guess.

## The Solution
**EvalLite Brain** is a lightweight AI model that turns the data you already have into **outcome measurement, ROI, and predictive social-impact forecasts** — in a plug-and-play Lite Dashboard built for non-technical teams.

## What You Get
- **ROI Calculator** — investment vs. measurable outcomes (uplift, reach, savings).
- **Predictive Evaluation** — will this project hit its KPIs? Know early.
- **Social Impact Intelligence (SII)** — Social Impact Index, Cost Per Outcome, Donor Accountability Score.
- **Lite Dashboard** — mobile-friendly, plug-and-play.
- **Scenario modelling** — test spend reallocations before you commit.
