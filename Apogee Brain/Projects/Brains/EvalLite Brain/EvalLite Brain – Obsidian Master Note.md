# EvalLite Brain – AI-Powered MEL & Impact Intelligence Platform

**Tags:** #Brains #SmartMart #ApogeeSKOPE #SaaS #MEL #AI #NGO

---

## Executive Summary

EvalLite Brain is an AI-powered Monitoring, Evaluation, and Learning (MEL) platform that helps NGOs, SMEs, development organizations, and impact-driven businesses measure outcomes, forecast results, optimize resource allocation, and demonstrate return on investment (ROI).

Unlike traditional M&E software that focuses primarily on historical reporting, EvalLite combines performance tracking, predictive analytics, and impact intelligence to help organizations answer:

- Are we achieving our targets?
- Which activities generate the greatest impact?
- Where should we reallocate resources?
- What outcomes are likely before the project ends?
- How can we improve donor confidence and accountability?

**Positioning Statement**

> "Donor-ready reporting and impact forecasting without enterprise complexity."

---

# Problem Statement

Organizations face several challenges:

### NGOs & Development Organizations

- Donor reporting is labor-intensive.
- Most reporting is backward-looking.
- Limited ability to predict program outcomes.
- Difficulty linking expenditure to measurable impact.
- Heavy reliance on Excel spreadsheets and consultants.

### SMEs & Mid-Sized Businesses

- Difficulty measuring ROI beyond revenue.
- Limited analytics expertise.
- Existing BI platforms are expensive and complex.
- Lack of impact measurement frameworks.

### Current Alternatives

- Excel / Google Sheets
- Power BI
- Tableau
- Dedicated M&E consultants
- Traditional M&E platforms

Most alternatives either:

- Require significant technical expertise,
- Focus on reporting rather than forecasting,
- Or are too expensive for smaller organizations.

# Core Product Modules

## 1. ROI Intelligence Engine

Tracks investments against measurable outcomes.

### Outputs

- ROI %
- Cost Savings
- Revenue Uplift
- Campaign Effectiveness
- Program Efficiency

### Example

Marketing Spend: KES 1,000,000

Revenue Increase: KES 3,500,000

ROI:

((3,500,000 − 1,000,000) ÷ 1,000,000) × 100

= 250%

---

## 2. Predictive Impact Engine

Uses machine learning to forecast whether projects will meet targets before completion.

### Capabilities

- KPI forecasting
- Outcome prediction
- Early warning alerts
- Resource optimization recommendations
- Trend analysis

### Example

Current vaccination campaign trajectory suggests:

- Target Achievement Probability: 73%
- Recommended budget reallocation: +20% WhatsApp outreach
- Predicted impact increase: 18%

---

# Key Risks

### Market Risk

Organizations may perceive EvalLite as "another dashboard."

Mitigation:

Position around decision-making and forecasting.

### Data Quality Risk

Poor input data reduces prediction accuracy.

Mitigation:

Data quality scoring.

### Adoption Risk

SMEs may resist analytics.

Mitigation:

Keep onboarding under 30 minutes.

### Competitive Risk

Larger M&E platforms may add AI features.

Mitigation:

Move faster and own the "Impact Intelligence" category.
