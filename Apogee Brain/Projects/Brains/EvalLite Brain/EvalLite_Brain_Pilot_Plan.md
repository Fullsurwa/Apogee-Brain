# EvalLite Brain – Pilot Plan

> The **HOW** of the Master Note's Go-To-Market **Phase 2 (Productized Service)**: convert service clients into EvalLite dashboard pilots, targeting **10–20 NGOs**, then transition to subscriptions (Phase 3).

Parent: [[EvalLite Brain – Obsidian Master Note]] · Driven by [[EvalLite_Brain_AI_Script]] · Validated in [[EvalLite_Brain_Test_Results]] · Data via [[EvalLite_Brain_Connectors]]

---

## Where this sits in the GTM (from Master Note)
1. **Phase 1 – Service-Led:** sell M&E consulting / ROI assessments; build datasets & case studies.
2. **Phase 2 – Productized Service:** introduce dashboards, sell pilots → **10–20 NGOs** *(this plan)*.
3. **Phase 3 – SaaS Scale:** transition recurring clients to subscriptions → 100 paying orgs.
4. **Phase 4 – Regional Expansion:** East Africa, multi-country donor programs.

---

## Objective
Prove, in **one donor-funded program over 8–12 weeks**, that EvalLite measures outcomes accurately and forecasts a reallocation that improves cost per outcome — producing donor-ready evidence and meeting the **≥ 85% forecast accuracy** bar.

## Scope (fixed)
- **1 NGO, 1 program**, agreed indicators (e.g. Cost per Beneficiary, Cost per Outcome, Conversion, SII).
- Data via existing sources only — Phase 1 connectors (Sheets/Excel/CSV), no new collection burden.
- Out of scope: custom models, multi-country rollups (reserved for Enterprise / Phase 4).

## Success / Acceptance Criteria
- Forecast accuracy ≥ 85% (Master Note product metric; tracked in [[EvalLite_Brain_Test_Results]]).
- ≥ 1 AI reallocation recommendation adopted and measured.
- Onboarding completed in **under 30 minutes** (adoption-risk guardrail).
- Donor report preparation time reduced (target −70%).

---

## Commercials
- **Pilot fee:** fixed (8–12 weeks), **credited against** the first subscription term on conversion.
- **Conversion target:** Pilot-to-Paid **≥ 50%** → **Pro tier KES 25,000/month** (Master Note Business Model).
- Optional **Services Layer** (KES 15,000–30,000/day) for setup, migration, donor-reporting support.
