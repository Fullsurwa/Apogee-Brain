# SmartMart Brain

## Overview
AI-driven indoor spatial intelligence platform for supermarkets and warehouses using Bluetooth 5.1 Angle of Arrival (AoA) phase-shift triangulation.

## Core Architecture
- Bluetooth AoA anchors and tracking tags
- Spatial triangulation and positioning engine
- Analytics layer for customer flow and inventory tracking

## Rollout Roadmap
- Phase 1: Core positioning engine validation
- Phase 2: Initial supermarket pilot deployment
- Phase 3: Commercial scaling
