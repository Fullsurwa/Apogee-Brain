## ⏱️ How It Works in 60 Seconds
1.  **Passive Capturing:** Shoppers use a color-coded BLE pin (or grab a tagged trolley/pallet), which locators passively map down to centimeter accuracy.
2.  **Natural Voice Command:** A floor manager spots a shift in traffic or an operational bottleneck, hits their headset link, and speaks naturally: *"Hi Apogee, there's a family on the middle aisle, please track that."*
3.  **Cognitive Execution:** The backend engine parses the text intent using advanced AI, updates the telemetry stream, modifies active target variables, and simultaneously triggers background webhooks to log the data.
4.  **Local Safety Net Validation:** Guaranteed uptime with local edge fallback architecture during network outages.
