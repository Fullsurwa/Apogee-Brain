## Design notes (for whoever builds the actual slide)
- **One page only.** If it doesn't fit, cut copy — don't shrink the font.
- **Hero line:** *"Open smarter. Shelf-level shopper insight — no cameras."*
- **Visual:** a simple store-floor heatmap graphic behind/beside the text sells it instantly.
- **Color cues:** use the demographic palette — 🔵 Men, 🔴 Women, 🟢 Families.
- **Single CTA:** *Book a pilot.* One action, bottom-right, with contact details.
- **Brand:** Apogee SKOPE LLP logo top or bottom corner.
