# SmartMart Brain — Objection-Handling Cheat Sheet

> Internal aid for the walkthrough call. Format: **Objection → Short answer → Proof/Bridge.** Acknowledge first, never argue.

---

## 🔒 Privacy & Compliance

**"Isn't this surveillance? Customers will hate being tracked."**
- **Answer:** There are no cameras and no facial recognition — we never know *who* anyone is. The system only sees an anonymous color (Men/Women/Family) moving through space.
- **Bridge:** It's the opposite of CCTV analytics. You get the insight *without* the privacy liability — which is itself a trust message you can put on signage.

**"What about the Data Protection Act?"**
- **Answer:** Because no personally identifiable information is ever collected or stored, the core system sits cleanly within Kenya's Data Protection Act (2019).
- **Proof:** Our pilot includes a DPA compliance checklist and architecture audit as a deliverable.

**"Do you store anything that could identify a shopper?"**
- **Answer:** No. We store anonymous paths tied to a reusable beacon, not a person. Pins are recycled between shoppers — there's no profile to build.

---

## 💰 Cost & ROI

**"This sounds expensive."**
- **Answer:** It's deliberately low-hardware — a handful of wall/pillar locators, not hundreds of ceiling sensors. That's a fraction of a camera-analytics or RSSI install.
- **Bridge:** And the pilot is fixed-scope, so you see ROI evidence *before* committing to a rollout.

**"How do I know it pays back?"**
- **Answer:** Two levers: better placement lifts sales per square metre, and predictive forecasting cuts stockouts *and* spoilage. In a price-competitive market, margin protection is the fast payback.
- **Bridge:** The pilot's success metrics include forecast-vs-actual validation on your own SKUs — real numbers, not our claims.

**"Can we just credit the pilot if we go ahead?"**
- **Answer:** Yes — we offer a risk-reversal that credits a portion of the pilot fee against rollout.

---

## 🛒 "Do shoppers actually take the pin?"

**"Won't people just ignore or refuse the pin?"**
- **Answer:** Two answers. First, even partial adoption gives statistically useful traffic patterns — we don't need 100%. Second, the stronger deployment embeds the tag directly in the basket/trolley, so the shopper does nothing extra; the cart *is* the tracker.
- **Bridge:** We pick the method per store during the survey.

**"Won't shoppers walk out with the pins?"**
- **Answer:** That's "the Forgetfulness Factor," and we plan for it: embedded trolley tags eliminate it for cart users, and a low-frequency EAS gate gives a polite chime as a backstop. The AI also logs incidents and flags high-loss exits/times.

**"Isn't carrying a pin annoying?"**
- **Answer:** It's a small beacon that slides into a pocket — or skip it entirely with a tagged trolley. Either way it's passive; the shopper just shops.

---

## ⚙️ Accuracy & Technical

**"Will metal shelving and crowds break the accuracy?"**
- **Answer:** This is exactly why we use Angle of Arrival, not signal strength. AoA measures *direction*, which survives attenuation far better than signal *strength* (RSSI). That's how we hold 10–30 cm where RSSI degrades to 5–10 m.
- **Proof:** Pilot validates ≤30 cm median error against reference tags on *your* floor.

**"We already have CCTV / a loyalty app — why this?"**
- **Answer:** CCTV tells you what a camera can see and carries privacy risk; a loyalty app only covers members who opt in and remember their phone. SmartMart Brain measures *everyone* anonymously, at shelf precision, with no privacy exposure. They're complementary, not competing.

**"Does it integrate with our POS / ERP?"**
- **Answer:** Not required for the pilot — it stands alone. POS/ERP integration for closed-loop forecasting comes in the rollout phase.

**"What if it doesn't work in our store?"**
- **Answer:** That's the entire point of a fixed-scope pilot with pre-agreed success metrics. If we don't hit them, you have a clean, low-cost off-ramp.

---

## 🕒 Timing / "Not now"

**"We're too busy opening — talk later."**
- **Answer:** The best time to install is *during* fit-out, before shelving is locked in — the data should *shape* your layout, not just audit it after the fact.
- **Bridge:** The survey takes a day and doesn't slow your opening.

**"Send me information and I'll review it."**
- **Answer:** Happy to — I'll send the one-pager now. Could we hold 30 minutes next week so I can tailor the pilot to your store size and layout? That's where the value gets concrete.
